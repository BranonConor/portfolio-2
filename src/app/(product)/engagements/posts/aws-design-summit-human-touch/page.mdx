### Our powers maybe superhuman, but the experiences we crave are still human

Lately, I've been sharing my goofy AI-building experiences on the web. Some of these experiences landed in front of design leadership at AWS's SSOG Transformers UX team, and I was kindly invited as a speaker to share my design engineering adventures at their recent AI design summit. The SSOG team is extremely AI-forward, working on super cutting edge AI design tooling and applications. They boast a "self-learning intelligence flywheel" via a three-part system to validate and scale their work:

- **Delight Lab** - their internal design research practice that senses and validates how humans and AI collaborate.
- **Design Brain** - a synthesis mechanism used to process research signals and user feedback.
- **Rhythm** - the mechanism that scales proven, validated UX patterns into a production design system built on AWS Core

So AI-building is top of mind for this talented bunch. 🤝

The more I build with AI, the more I realize what I bring to the work: human judgment, character, and craft. In this new age of AI-charged superpowers, designers and engineers can will almost anything into existence - and yet, our humanness matters more than ever in this new paradigm. I hope you enjoy the talk!
