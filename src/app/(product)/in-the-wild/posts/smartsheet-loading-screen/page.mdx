### What happened

On August 28, 2025, Smartsheet [announced that its new app loading screen was generally available](https://www.smartsheet.com/content-center/new-app-loading-screen-now-generally-available) across the platform - the splash experience every user sees while the app boots up.

Loading screens are one of those surfaces that live in a gap: they're highly visible, they set the tone for the whole product, and yet they rarely have a clear owner. That's exactly the kind of surface I love to take on.

### Where I fit in

This one is a good example of what design engineering can do when someone owns a problem end-to-end:

- **Design** - I partnered with a product designer to design the new loading experience, and I focused on the **animations and motion** - the timing, easing, and feel of the whole sequence.
- **Implementation** - I then **fully implemented it myself**, front to back, and drove it all the way to general availability.

Being able to work across the entire design → implementation stack meant this legacy surface could be brought into the modern age without waiting on a hand-off between disciplines. Bringing neglected, high-visibility surfaces up to modern standards is a huge, quiet UX win - and it's some of the most satisfying work I do.
